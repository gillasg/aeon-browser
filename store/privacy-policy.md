# Graft Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-27

Graft is a Chrome extension and a small program on your own computer. Together they let an AI
model you choose do tasks in your Chrome. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to
your data.

## The short version

- Your data stays on your computer. The extension talks only to the Graft program installed on
  the same computer, through Chrome's built-in native messaging.
- Graft runs no server. We do not receive your pages, chats, passwords, cookies or files.
- Graft has no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no telemetry.
- We never sell, rent or share your data with anyone.

## What Graft reads, and why

To do a task you ask for, Graft can read and act on the pages it works on: page text, form
fields, screenshots, network requests, cookies, downloads and tabs. This is used only to carry
out your request, on your computer.

Chats, settings, saved site maps, Boosts and run history are stored locally, in Chrome's
extension storage and in Graft's data folder on your computer.

## AI providers

You pick the AI provider and connect your own account or key. When a task runs, Graft sends that
provider what it needs for the task (your message and the parts of pages the model asks to see).
That traffic goes straight from your computer to the provider you chose, under that provider's
own privacy policy. Graft does not pass it through any server of ours.

## Your controls

- You see every step Graft takes, in the side panel and with the on-screen cursor, and you can
  stop a run at any time.
- Extra permissions (history, bookmarks, clipboard reading, site settings, privacy settings, tab
  recording) are asked for only when a task first needs them, and you can say no.
- You can delete chats and history from the side panel, or delete Graft's data folder.

## Uninstalling

Remove the extension from `chrome://extensions`. Then uninstall the Graft program from your
computer and delete its data folder. Nothing remains on any server, because none was used.

## Children

Graft is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address with a new date.
